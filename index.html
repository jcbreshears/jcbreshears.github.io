04 About

Hi, I'm Justin.

I build websites and software for small businesses — usually as the only person on the project, which means the person you talk to is the person writing the code.

I care about the unglamorous half: that it loads quickly on a phone on a bad connection, that it works with a keyboard and a screen reader, that the forms actually reach your inbox, and that you can still run your business if I'm on holiday. I'd rather ship something small that works than something impressive that doesn't.

I don't use drag-and-drop page builders or bolt on plugins to cover gaps. It's slower to start and much better to live with — nothing to un-pick later, no monthly licence for a feature you already paid for.
